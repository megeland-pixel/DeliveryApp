Universal Spiral Air — Customer Text Message Program

How We Use Text Messaging

Universal Spiral Air sends one-time SMS notifications to customers who have provided a phone number in connection with a delivery order. These messages notify you that your driver is on the way and provide an estimated arrival time.

How You Opt In

You provide your consent to receive SMS notifications by supplying a phone number at the time your order is placed or when arranging delivery with our team. No separate sign-up is required. By providing your number, you agree to receive delivery status messages from Universal Spiral Air.

Message Types & Frequency

  • Delivery ETA notifications (e.g., “Your driver is approximately 15 minutes away”)
  • One message per delivery, sent only on the day of your scheduled delivery
  • Messages are sent by our delivery driver directly

Message and data rates may apply. Frequency varies based on your delivery schedule. This program is for transactional delivery notifications only — we do not send marketing messages.

How to Opt Out

Reply STOP to any message at any time to unsubscribe. You will receive a confirmation and no further messages will be sent. You may also contact us directly to remove your number.

Privacy

Your phone number is used solely to send delivery notifications related to your order. We do not share, sell, or rent your phone number to third parties. Message history is retained only as long as necessary for operational purposes.
